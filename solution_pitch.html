The Challenge: The Telecom Reality

  • Acoustic Degradation: Indian voice agents operate over 8 kHz band-limited, lossy telephony networks with heavy traffic, babble, and carrier compression.
  • Standard Metrics Fail: Word Error Rate (WER) treats valid orthographic variations (e.g., "₹5 लाख" vs "5,00,000 रुपये") as outright errors.
  • Manual QA Bottleneck: Recording, crowdsourcing, and annotating test calls takes weeks, costs thousands, and cannot easily re-test new model checkpoints.

The Solution: Closed-Loop Kasauti

  • Synthetic Ground Truth: Synthesizes seed sentences containing known phone numbers, PINs, rupee amounts, and timestamps using Gnani's Timbre TTS.
  • PSTN Channel Emulation: Degrades audio through 300–3,400 Hz filtering, 8 kHz downsampling, G.711 μ-law quantization, and calibrated noise (20 dB down to 0 dB).
  • Semantic Value Scorer: Normalizes Indic numeric ASTs to judge exact business meaning rather than superficial spelling.

Key Engineering Discoveries

  • The 0 dB Noise Cliff: Prisma v3.0 experiences heavy digit substitutions under intense noise (turning 1 into 8), whereas v2.5 preserves sequence length.
  • Attribution Isolation: Identified a clean-audio bug in Timbre TTS where digit 8 was repeated 3 times instead of 4, proving STT was blameless.
  • Fragility Breakdown: 82% of all failures occur in digit strings (phones/PINs); currency amounts and times are reinforced by surrounding context.
  • Hallucination Detection: Caught low-SNR acoustic stress triggering models to hallucinate phantom tokens (predominantly "था").
